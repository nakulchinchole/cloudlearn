College Cloud Computing project

Study material, delivered from the edge.

CloudLearn is a student resource portal. It shows how files stored in Amazon S3 can reach students quickly and securely through the CloudFront CDN.

Browse resourcesSee the architecture

The problem

Students need notes and cheat sheets that load fast from anywhere and are not lost when one server fails.

The idea

Store files once in S3, let CloudFront keep copies near students, and protect everything with HTTPS.

The result

A working portal with real sample PDFs, plus simulators that teach hits, misses and origins.
